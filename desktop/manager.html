First-run notes

Your database, TLS key and server logs are stored in this operating-system user's application data folder. The app creates a local development certificate automatically. It is suitable for local testing only; other devices need a trusted certificate and a properly configured network address.

This application does not run as a background service after you quit it. Keep the manager open while hosting. Do not close the app while other people are actively chatting.

Text chat only · TLS required · Do not share your private key or application data folder.
